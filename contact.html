Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things people ask us most.

Does LaslesVPN keep logs of my activity?

The Free plan keeps no traffic logs at all. The Standard and Premium plans keep basic connection logs to help us fix problems.

How many devices can I use?

Every plan works on all of your devices, including phones, tablets and computers.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can move up or down between the Free, Standard and Premium plans at any time from your account.

What does Connect Anywhere mean?

It lets you choose a server in any of our locations around the world, so you can connect from wherever you are travelling.

LaslesVPN is a private virtual network that has unique features and has high security.
